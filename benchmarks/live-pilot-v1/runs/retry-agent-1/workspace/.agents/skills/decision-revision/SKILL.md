---
name: decision-revision
description: Reassess a saved product decision when new evidence, a retracted source or changed assumptions may invalidate its grounds; preserve the original decision history.
---

Start with `resume`. Identify the prior source and claims affected by the new information. Import the new source with a new ID; keep the original snapshot.

Use `source supersede` for a corrected edition or `source withdraw` for unreliable evidence. Inspect its affected sources and claims. Do not replace unrelated evidence merely because it is older. For contradictory but still valid sources, keep both active, add the counterevidence and explicitly revise research.

Trace the impact through findings, alternatives, the decision and its commitments. Explain what changed, what did not, and why. Add new claims, retract disproved claims with a reason, then commit updated research and options. A source marked withdrawn cannot support a new active claim.

Reopen the human decision gate when the choice or its grounds materially change. Do not sign for the decision owner. Update the strategy and flag commitments tied to the old decision hash. Finish with audit and export; failed audit means a draft, not an updated approved recommendation.

If the evidence is inconclusive, preserve uncertainty and propose a discriminating check. Do not silently repeat prior external actions. Monitoring occurs when this procedure is invoked; no background watcher is implied.
